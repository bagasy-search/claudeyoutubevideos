Glycerin, sometimes spelled glycerine, and sometimes called glycerol, is a clear, thick, slightly sweet liquid. It has no smell. It feels a bit like syrup between your fingers. A humectant like glycerin works on exactly that problem. It helps the top layer hold water. And water is what makes skin feel soft and flexible instead of tight and brittle.
