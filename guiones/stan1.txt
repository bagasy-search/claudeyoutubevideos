Trick number one. Buy it thick, or don't bother. One and a half inches, at the least. Two is better. Most people grab the thin steaks in the package because they look like a better deal. Three-quarters of an inch, a nice price, and it's ruined before you even turn on the stove.
Here's why. A thin steak has no middle. By the time the outside gets a good brown crust, the heat has already gone all the way through, and the whole thing is gray. Trick number two. Dry it, and salt it the night before. This is the one people don't believe until they taste it once. You pat the steak dry with paper towels. Every side. Then you salt it.
