[S03] Let's talk about why boiled green beans turn gray.
[S03] That bright green color comes from chlorophyll. It's what makes plants green. And chlorophyll is fragile. When you first cook a green bean, for the first few minutes, it actually gets brighter green. You've seen that. That's the air in the bean escaping, so you can see the color better. But if you keep cooking it, especially in a covered pot of water, the acids that come out of the beans go into the water and attack the chlorophyll. After about seven or eight minutes, the green fades to that dull army-olive color. And it never comes back.
[S03] The other problem is the water itself. A pot of water pulls out the flavor, just like it did with the potatoes. And the beans soak it up. So they're soft, watery, and taste like nothing.
