🍲 POTATO, ONION & EGG SUPPER ($0.65 a plate) — page 41 of the book
• 3 Tbsp bacon fat or oil
• 3 medium potatoes, thinly sliced
• 1 onion, sliced
• 1 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp pepper, 1 tsp paprika
• 4 eggs
• Hot sauce or ketchup
1. Heat the fat in a 12-inch skillet over medium. Add the potatoes in layers with the onion and half the salt.
2. Cover and cook 10 minutes, then uncover, turn, and cook 10 minutes more until tender and browned in spots.
3. Season with the rest of the salt, pepper and paprika.
4. Make 4 hollows, crack in the eggs, cover and cook 4 to 5 minutes until the whites set.
Ole's trick: Cover the potatoes for the first 10 minutes. They steam tender, then brown when you take the lid off.

🍲 CHICKEN NOODLE SOUP FROM LEG QUARTERS ($0.85 a bowl) — page 30 of the book
• 2 chicken leg quarters
• 12 cups water, 1 Tbsp salt
• 2 onions, 3 carrots, 3 celery ribs, chopped
• 1 bay leaf, 1 tsp thyme, 1/2 tsp pepper
• 6 oz egg noodles
• Parsley
1. Simmer the chicken in the water with the salt, bay and half an onion, 50 minutes. Skim the foam.
2. Lift out the chicken, pull the meat off and chop it. Return the bones for 20 minutes more, then strain them out.
3. Add the vegetables and thyme. Simmer 15 minutes.
4. Add the noodles and chicken and simmer 8 minutes more.
5. Taste for salt and pepper. Add parsley.
Ole's trick: Cook the noodles in the soup only for what you will eat tonight. For leftovers, keep the broth and cook fresh noodles each time.

🍲 HALUSKI: CABBAGE & NOODLES ($0.80 a plate) — page 8 of the book
• 1 medium green cabbage, cored and sliced
• 2 onions, sliced
• 6 Tbsp butter (or half bacon fat)
• 12 oz wide egg noodles
• 1 1/2 tsp salt, 1 tsp black pepper
• 4 slices bacon or 6 oz kielbasa (optional)
1. If using bacon or kielbasa, brown it in a big skillet and set aside, keeping the fat.
2. Melt the butter in the fat, add the onions and cook 10 minutes.
3. Add the cabbage and salt. Cook over medium 20 to 25 minutes, turning now and then, until soft, sweet and browned in spots.
4. Meanwhile boil the noodles in salted water and drain.
5. Toss the noodles with the cabbage, the meat and plenty of pepper.
Ole's trick: Do not rush the cabbage. Low and slow until it is golden and tastes almost like caramel: that is the whole dish.
