🍲 REFRIED BEANS IN LARD ($0.30 a serving) — page 25 of the book
• 4 cups cooked pinto beans with 1 cup of their liquid
• 3 Tbsp lard or bacon fat
• 1/2 onion, finely chopped
• 1 clove garlic
• 1/2 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp cumin
1. Heat the fat in a large skillet over medium and cook the onion 5 minutes until golden. Add the garlic for 30 seconds.
2. Add the beans and half the liquid. They will sizzle.
3. Mash with a potato masher right in the pan, adding liquid until they are as smooth as you like.
4. Cook 5 to 8 minutes, stirring, until they thicken and pull from the pan.
5. Season with salt and cumin. They thicken more as they cool.
Ole's trick: Keep some bean liquid aside. Refried beans set up as they stand: stir a spoon of liquid in before serving and they come back creamy.

🍲 FIVE-MINUTE BEAN & BACON SOUP ($0.50 a bowl) — page 38 of the book
• 2 cups leftover cooked beans
• 1 1/2 cups bean broth or water
• 2 slices bacon, chopped
• 1/2 onion, chopped
• 1/2 tsp paprika
• Salt, pepper, splash of vinegar
1. Fry the bacon in a small pot until crisp. Lift out half for the top.
2. Cook the onion in the fat 4 minutes, add the paprika.
3. Add the beans and broth, bring to a simmer, and mash or blend until thick.
4. Season with salt, pepper and vinegar. Top with the bacon.
Ole's trick: Never pour bean broth down the drain. Keep it in a jar: it is the base of this soup and the best gravy you will ever thicken.

🍲 OLD-TIME NAVY BEAN PIE ($0.35 a slice) — page 42 of the book
• 1 unbaked 9-inch pie crust
• 2 cups cooked navy beans, well drained
• 1/2 cup melted butter
• 1 cup sugar
• 2 eggs
• 1 can (12 oz) evaporated milk
• 1 Tbsp flour
• 1 tsp cinnamon, 1/2 tsp nutmeg, 1 tsp vanilla, pinch salt
1. Heat the oven to 375°F.
2. Blend the beans and butter until completely smooth, with no bits of skin.
3. Blend in everything else until silky.
4. Pour into the crust.
5. Bake 50 to 60 minutes, until set at the edges with a slight wobble in the middle. Cool completely before slicing.
Ole's trick: Blend until there is not one fleck of bean skin left. Smooth is what makes people ask for the recipe instead of asking what is in it.

⚠️ Always boil dried beans hard for 10 full minutes first (kidney beans especially). A slow cooker alone never gets hot enough.
