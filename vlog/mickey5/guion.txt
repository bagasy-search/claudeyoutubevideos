[THREE] Pat the turkey dry. Then slide your fingers under the skin of the breast and the thighs, gently, and rub about half the salt right on the meat. The rest goes all over the outside. Then put it on a rack in a pan, uncovered, in the refrigerator, for one to three days.

[FOUR] Trick number four. This is the old Boston trick, and it's the one that fixes the problem from the start of this video. Ice on the breast. The breast is done at about one sixty, the thighs want one seventy-five. So you want the breast to start colder than the legs.
[FOUR] An hour before it goes in the oven, take the turkey out of the refrigerator and set it on the counter. Fill two zipper bags with ice, and lay them right on top of the breast. Cover the bags with a towel. The legs sit out and warm up, the breast stays ice cold.
