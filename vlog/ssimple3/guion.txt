[THREE] Number three is the eyes. This is the one everybody gets wrong. Old people walk looking down at their feet, because they are afraid of falling. But when you look at your feet, your head goes forward, your back bends, and you are already falling a little bit with every step. My mother said, look at the third telephone pole. Your eyes see the crack in the sidewalk long before your feet get there, and your head stays over your body, where it belongs. Only two places get a look down: a curb and a stair. [FOUR] Number four is the heel. In Japanese we say, kakato kara. It means, from the heel.
