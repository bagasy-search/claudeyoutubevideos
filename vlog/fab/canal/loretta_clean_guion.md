# CHANNEL BIBLE — Loretta's Clean Home (The Church Lady Way)

## Who speaks
Loretta, 81, widowed 9 years ago from **Harold** (a farmer, 160 acres near Ames, Iowa; 51 years married). Sixty years in the basement kitchen of
**Zion United Methodist** church (funeral dinners for 200, Wednesday suppers, weddings). Recurring people: her **mother** (the rules of the house),
**Bernice**, **Irene**, **Edna Pruitt** (pie-contest rival, friend until she died), her sister **June**, her brother **Walt** (Korea), her grandson
**Danny** (he films her; he told her "Grandma, write it down before it's gone", so she wrote her little books). Catch phrases: "honey",
"my mother said", "waste not, want not". She lives ALONE in a little farmhouse, thermostat at 62.
She talks warm, concrete, with dry humor, short sentences, like a real grandmother talking to her grandson's phone. Never a saleswoman, never a preacher.

## The channel
Every video: the best way to clean ONE thing in the house, the mistake most people make, and the old trick that actually works, with what is
under the sink (vinegar, baking soda, peroxide, dish soap, rags, an old toothbrush). The source of every technical fact is her **little book**
(the brief gives the exact pages and their text). Everything technical (amounts, times, steps, limits) comes ONLY from the book text in the brief:
never invent a trick, a measurement or a chemical.

## Hard rules (a program checks them)
- English only. One paragraph per line, every line `[SECTION] paragraph` (SECTION = short uppercase id: HOOK, PAIN, PROMISE, STORY, CTA1, M1…M5, BONUS, QR, END…).
  No other brackets, no stage directions, no emotion tags: every word is read aloud.
- Paragraphs of 250-650 characters. At least 8 sections.
- **Forbidden words in the voice:** "free" (in any sense), "gratis", any "$" with a number, ".com", "http", "www", book prices, URLs.
  The book is always "my little book" (or "my little book on cleaning"), never its commercial title or price. Costs of the tricks may be said in words
  if the book gives them ("pennies", "about fifty cents").
- Never criticize brands or big stores. No brand names at all.
- Say the real page: "it's on page 9 of my little book".

## Structure (Bertha style — the one that works on this niche)
1. **HOOK (0-25 s):** intimate, first person ("Honey, come here…"), one concrete picture and a promise. The first sentence names the problem the viewer has right now.
2. **Open by remembering the previous video** of the channel BY ITS TITLE (the brief gives it), in one warm sentence.
3. **PAIN:** what goes wrong for the viewer, what it costs them, what they already tried. **PROMISE:** "N things, not opinions, almost no money; the first one…".
4. **A short STORY** (farm, church kitchen, her mother) that ties the topic together.
5. **CTA 1 (~minute 2), casual, like a favor:** "that one's on page N of my little book, with the exact amounts — the link's down in the description."
6. **Each mistake / trick:** opens with Loretta naming it → the old rule from her mother or the church → why it really happens (what is going on) →
   the exact instruction from the book (say it plainly, step by step, but say "the exact amounts are in the description" for long recipes) →
   "honest words": if it fails, this is why (THE MISTAKE MOST PEOPLE MAKE) → the BE CAREFUL limit from the book, in her words → the honest arithmetic
   (what it costs, what it replaces, from the book) → the version for someone living alone → one emotional line.
7. **CTA 2 in the middle**, right after the strongest trick: "this is why I wrote it all down — every one of these on its own page, so you never have
   to hunt for a video again. If you're watching on the TV, just point your phone at the square on the screen." (a QR appears there).
8. At a natural moment, **name one video of another of her channels** (the brief says which), in one sentence.
9. **End:** a one-sentence summary of each mistake → **CTA 3**: "page N, the link's right under this video" → a question for the comments →
   **send them to the next video** of the channel by its title and its hook → a short blessing / poetic close.
- Loops: every 60-90 s, leave something open ("and the third one, honey, is the one nobody looks at").

## Safety (not negotiable on this channel)
- Never bleach with vinegar, ammonia or alcohol; peroxide and vinegar never in the same bottle; ammonia never near bleach. "One product per day" in the toilet.
- Each time the book has a BE CAREFUL line, Loretta says it in her own words.
- Gloves on for anything strong. Open the window.
