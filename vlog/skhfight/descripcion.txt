THE 11 RULES
1. Find your 10%: walk the house once a year with a cup of tea and name the things someone would cry over.
2. Ask each child alone, never at the table: "When you think of this house, what is the one thing you see?"
3. Give every thing its story: a small washi tag with who, when and one short story.
4. Give with warm hands: give some treasures now, while you can see their face.
5. Split the sets: five teacups, three homes, one table at New Year.
6. One watch, two sons: the parent decides, and the parent says why.
7. One notebook, written by hand: what, where, who, and why.
8. Show everything, hide nothing: a showing once a year, not a surprise in a drawer.
9. The ones not at the table: include the husbands, the wives and the grandchildren.
10. Let go of the 90% now, so the 10% can shine.
11. Say it out loud to each child: this is yours, this is why, and I love all of you the same.
